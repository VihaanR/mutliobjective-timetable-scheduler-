1. OVERVIEW & DEFINITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hard constraints represent non-negotiable physical, structural, and institutional
invariants. 

A timetable is legally valid (FEASIBLE) if and only if:
    TOTAL HARD VIOLATIONS = 0

Any timetable with even a single hard violation is rejected by the solver 
comparator regardless of its soft cost score.

3. SUMMARY OF HARD VIOLATION IMPACT
================================================================================
* An infeasible timetable has hard_violations > 0.
* In scoring comparison: 
    better(A, B) -> A is chosen if hard_violations(A) < hard_violations(B).
* If hard_violations(A) == hard_violations(B) == 0, then and only then is the 
  secondary tie-breaker (soft_cost) evaluated.
